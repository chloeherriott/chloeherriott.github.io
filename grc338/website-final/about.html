Chloe’s Cupcakes is a small bakery based in San Luis Obispo, created from Chloe’s love for baking and sharing sweet treats with others. What started as baking cupcakes for friends and family quickly turned into a dream of opening a business that brings a little extra joy to the community.

At Chloe’s Cupcakes, every cupcake is baked fresh with high-quality ingredients and lots of creativity. From classic flavors like chocolate and vanilla to fun seasonal ideas, each cupcake is made to be both delicious and memorable. We believe the best desserts are the ones that feel homemade, comforting, and a little bit special.

Chloe’s Cupcakes is all about bringing people together through simple, sweet moments. Whether you’re celebrating a birthday, picking up a treat for a friend, or just stopping by because you’re craving something sweet, we want every cupcake to make your day a little better. Our goal is simple: to make people smile and add a little sweetness to everyday life in San Luis Obispo.
